# Signature Scent Launch

Sarkar Perfume has a store at sarkar.store. In this activity, you will imagine and launch your own

signature fragrance under the Sarkar Perfume brand. You will keep the existing Sarkar packaging exactly

as it is. Your job is not to redesign the packaging. Your job is to create a landing page that sells your

fragrance idea, then make that landing page fast, clean and mobile friendly using AI tools.

You will use an AI website builder called Lovable to build the page, publish it, test its speed, fix problems,

and if needed, connect it to GitHub and use an AI coding agent called Codex to finish the job. By the end,

you will have a live, published landing page that you built and improved yourself.

Reference store: https://sarkar.store/



1. Visit sarkar.store and look at how the brand presents its perfumes, packaging, pricing and tone of

voice.

2. Pick or invent your own fragrance name, notes (for example citrus, oud, floral, woody) and a one line

brand story.

3. Do not change the Sarkar packaging design. Your fragrance should look like it belongs on the same

shelf as the existing Sarkar products.

Include at minimum a hero section with the fragrance name and tagline, a section on fragrance notes,

a product image area, pricing or a call to action button, and a footer.

3. Keep the Sarkar packaging visuals intact if you are using product images. Focus your creativity on the

page layout, copywriting and offer, not the packaging.
